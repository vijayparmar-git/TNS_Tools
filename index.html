Frequently asked questions

What is an SEO website audit?

It checks the technical setup, on-page elements, content and speed of a site to find problems that hold back its visibility in search engines.

What are AEO and GEO?

AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) structures content so search and AI assistants can lift clear answers from it. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) prepares your site to be found, read and cited by generative AI systems.

What is the difference between SEO, AEO and GEO?

SEO earns rankings in traditional search results. AEO earns direct answers and featured snippets. GEO earns visibility inside AI-generated answers.

How does the audit work?

You enter a public URL. The tool fetches the page, robots.txt, sitemap and AI files, runs each check on what it actually observes, scores the results and explains how to fix every issue.

Does allowing AI crawlers guarantee AI recommendations?

No. Crawler access is a prerequisite, not a guarantee. This audit measures technical readiness only, not whether an AI platform recommends your business.

Is llms.txt mandatory for Google rankings?

No. llms.txt is an optional, emerging convention and not a Google ranking factor. The tool reports it as an optional signal.

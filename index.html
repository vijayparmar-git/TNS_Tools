Frequently asked questions

What is Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)?

AEO structures your content so search and AI assistants can lift a clear answer from it: direct opening answers, question headings, FAQ sections and structured data.

What is Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)?

GEO prepares your site to be found, read and cited by generative AI systems: crawler access, clear brand and entity information, and extractable, trustworthy content.

Does allowing AI crawlers guarantee AI recommendations?

No. Access is a prerequisite, not a guarantee. This audit measures technical readiness only.

Is llms.txt mandatory for Google rankings?

No. llms.txt is an optional, emerging convention and not a Google ranking factor. We report it as an optional signal.
